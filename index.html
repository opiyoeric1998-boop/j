Ask Us Directly

What makes New Jets Comprehensive School stand out?

We blend strong academics with modern learning resources, passionate teachers, and a warm family environment — with a genuine international-school standard delivered right here in Kisumu.

Do you communicate regularly with parents?

Absolutely! We keep parents updated through scheduled meetings, direct messages, and weekly progress reports so you always know how your child is growing.

What co-curricular activities are available?

From music, art, sports, cooking, debate, science fair, environmental club, young entrepreneurs, fashion & modelling, to Tae Kwon Do — we help your child discover new passions and skills.

How do I enrol my child?

It's simple! Visit the school, call our office, or download the admission form from our Fees page. Our admissions team will guide you through every step.

What curriculum do you follow?

We proudly implement Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), using fun, hands-on methods that help every child discover their talents and develop genuine competencies for life.

What ages do you admit?

We welcome learners from Playgroup (age 2–3) through PP1, PP2, and all the way to Grade 4, giving children a strong, joyful start to their education.

How do you keep children safe?

Safety is our highest priority. Secure grounds, clean classrooms, trained first-aiders, strict visitor policies, and a comprehensive child protection framework are all in place at all times.
