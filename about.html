Our Journey

A School Born From Vision

Jets Elementary School was founded in August 2023 by its visionary founder, Madam Diannah Omariba, with a clear mission: to provide a nurturing, learning-centred environment where every child has the opportunity to grow, discover, and succeed. Jets opened its doors with an initial enrolment of 50 learners.

Despite being a young institution, the school quickly demonstrated its commitment to excellence. Within just two months of operation, Jets proudly celebrated its first graduating learner — a significant milestone in its early journey.

Today, Jets Elementary stands as a growing, international-minded institution committed to excellence, inclusivity, and preparing young learners to thrive in a global community. Our holistic approach ensures true learning extends beyond textbooks and classrooms.

Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles that shape everything we do at Jets Elementary.

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, student-centred education that empowers children to become confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizens. We strive to create a learning environment that celebrates diversity, encourages critical thinking, and fosters a lifelong love of learning.

Through innovative teaching methods and a holistic curriculum, we develop not only academic excellence but also strong character and social responsibility in every student.

Our Vision

We envision a school where every child discovers their unique talents, develops strong character, and acquires the skills needed to thrive in an ever-changing world. Our vision is to be a leading elementary school known for academic excellence and a strong sense of community.

We aim to create future leaders equipped with knowledge, empathy, and the resilience to make positive contributions to society.
