Our Journey

A School Born From Vision

New Jets Comprehensive School was founded in August 2023 with a clear and passionate mission: to provide a nurturing, learning-centred environment where every child has the opportunity to grow, discover, and succeed.

We opened our doors with an initial enrolment of 50 learners. Despite being a young institution, the school quickly demonstrated its commitment to excellence — within just two months of operation, we proudly celebrated our first graduating learner.

Today, New Jets Comprehensive School stands as a growing, international-minded institution committed to excellence, inclusivity, and preparing young learners to thrive in a global community. Our holistic approach ensures that true learning extends far beyond textbooks and classrooms.

Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles that shape everything we do at New Jets Comprehensive School.

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, student-centred education that empowers children to become confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizens. We strive to create a learning environment that celebrates diversity, encourages critical thinking, and fosters a lifelong love of learning.

Through innovative teaching and a holistic curriculum, we develop not only academic excellence but also strong character and social responsibility in every student.

Our Vision

We envision a school where every child discovers their unique talents, develops strong character, and acquires the skills needed to thrive in an ever-changing world — a leading education center known for both academic excellence and a strong community spirit.

We aim to create future leaders equipped with knowledge, empathy, and the resilience to make positive contributions to society and the world.
