About Jets Elementary School

Nurturing young minds with excellence, compassion, and innovation for a brighter future

Our Story

Jets Elementary School was founded in August 2023 by its visionary founder, Madam Diannah Omariba, with a clear mission: to provide a nurturing, learning-centered environment where every child has the opportunity to grow, discover, and succeed. Jets opened its doors with an initial enrollment of 50 learners.

Despite being a young institution, the school quickly demonstrated its commitment to excellence. Within just two months of operation, Jets proudly celebrated its first graduating learner, marking a significant milestone in its early journey.

Today, Jets Elementary stands as a growing international-minded institution, committed to excellence, inclusivity, and preparing young learners to thrive in a global community. What makes Jets Elementary unique is our holistic approach to education. We believe that true learning extends beyond textbooks and classrooms.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that shape our educational approach

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, student-centered education that empowers children to become confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizens. We strive to create a learning environment that celebrates diversity, encourages critical thinking, and fosters a lifelong love of learning.

Through innovative teaching methods and a holistic curriculum, we aim to develop not only academic excellence but also strong character and social responsibility in every student.

Our Vision

We envision a school where every child discovers their unique talents, develops strong character, and acquires the skills needed to thrive in an ever-changing world. Our vision is to be a leading elementary school known for academic excellence, innovative teaching methods, and a strong sense of community.

We aim to create future leaders who are equipped with knowledge, empathy, and the resilience to make positive contributions to society.
