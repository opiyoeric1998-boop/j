Why Our Programmes

Safe Learning Beyond School Hours

Our Learning Programmes provide a structured, safe, and stimulating environment for children. We understand the challenges working parents face — and we're here to support both you and your child.

At Jets Elementary, our programmes aren't just supervision — they're an extension of our educational philosophy, creating opportunities for social interaction, skill development, and creative expression.

Safety & Security

Professional staff ensure your child's wellbeing at all times with secure, supervised facilities.

Skill Development

Activities designed to build confidence, critical thinking, and new abilities in a supportive setting.

Social Growth

Guided interactions that build friendships, teamwork, and essential social skills.

What We Offer

Our Learning Programmes

Each programme is carefully designed to nurture a different aspect of your child's development.

Physical

Sports & Play

Active games and team sports that promote physical fitness, coordination, and social skills in a safe, structured environment.

  • Builds teamwork and sportsmanship
  • Improves physical health and coordination
  • Teaches discipline and fair play
  • Encourages healthy competition
Technology

Computer Learning & Robotics

Introduction to technology, coding basics, and simple robotics projects — making digital learning fun and accessible for young minds.

  • Develops problem-solving skills
  • Introduces basic programming concepts
  • Encourages creativity and innovation
  • Builds confidence with technology
Strategy

Chess Game

The timeless game of strategy taught in an engaging, age-appropriate way — children learn to think ahead and develop mental focus.

  • Enhances critical thinking skills
  • Improves concentration and patience
  • Teaches planning and strategy
  • Boosts memory and cognitive abilities
Aquatics

Swimming

Supervised swimming lessons and water safety instruction by certified instructors ensuring a safe, enjoyable aquatic experience.

  • Teaches essential water safety skills
  • Builds confidence in the water
  • Improves overall physical fitness
  • Develops coordination and strength
Life Skills

Cooking

Simple, safe cooking activities that teach basic kitchen skills and nutrition — children prepare healthy snacks while understanding food concepts.

  • Teaches practical life skills
  • Encourages healthy eating habits
  • Develops math and measuring skills
  • Boosts creativity and confidence
Communication

Debate

Age-appropriate discussion and public speaking activities that help children express their ideas clearly and respectfully.

  • Improves communication skills
  • Builds confidence in public speaking
  • Teaches respectful disagreement
  • Develops critical thinking and reasoning
Martial Arts

Taekwondo

Martial arts training focused on discipline, respect, and self-control rather than aggression — character development first.

  • Promotes self-discipline and focus
  • Teaches respect for self and others
  • Builds confidence and self-esteem
  • Improves physical coordination
