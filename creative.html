Why Creative Arts?

Every Child is an Artist

At New Jets Comprehensive School, we believe creativity is not a talent reserved for a few — it is a capacity present in every child, waiting to be nurtured. Our Creative Arts programme is woven through every school day, not treated as an optional extra.

Through visual arts, music, drama, movement, and craft, children develop confidence, emotional intelligence, and a distinctive creative voice that enriches everything they do — academically and socially.

What We Cover

Areas of Creative Arts

Six interconnected disciplines that develop every dimension of a child's creative self.

Drawing & Painting

Exploring colours, shapes, and techniques to express ideas visually using various mediums and tools.

Music

Singing, rhythm activities, introduction to instruments, and music appreciation from diverse cultures.

Drama

Role-play, storytelling, puppet shows, and simple performances to build confidence and communication.

Craft

Hands-on activities using recycled materials, paper, clay, and textiles to create 3D artworks.

Movement & Dance

Creative dance, coordination exercises, expressive movements, and rhythm-based physical activities.

Collaboration

Group murals, ensemble performances, and shared projects that build teamwork and diverse perspectives.

Why It Matters

Importance of Creative Arts Education

Arts education develops the whole child — cognitively, emotionally, and socially.

Builds Imagination & Self-Expression

A safe space to explore thoughts and feelings. Children develop their unique voice without judgement.

Encourages Teamwork & Confidence

Group performances and collaborative projects teach children to work together and share their work.

Develops Fine Motor Skills

Cutting, drawing, painting, and modelling strengthen hand-eye coordination essential for writing.

Promotes Cultural Appreciation

Exposure to diverse art forms fosters global awareness, tolerance, and cultural appreciation.

Enhances Academic Performance

Arts education improves focus, memory, and problem-solving across all subject areas.

Fosters Emotional Intelligence

Through arts, children learn to identify, express, and manage emotions — building self-awareness.

By Grade Level

Learning Outcomes by Level

A carefully structured progression from foundational exploration to creative expression.

PP1 – PP2: Foundational Exploration

Early childhood creative development through play and sensory exploration

Colour & Shape Recognition

Exploring primary colours through finger painting and collage

Rhythm & Song

Singing simple songs and nursery rhymes with basic percussion

Role-play & Imagination

Basic role-play and puppet shows to develop social skills

Free Drawing

Free drawing with crayons, markers, and chalk on varied surfaces

Movement Coordination

Simple rhythm activities to develop body awareness

Sensory Development

Introduction to textures through sensory play with varied materials

Grade 1 – 2: Skill Development

Building foundational skills in various art forms with guided instruction

Mixed Media Art

Creative drawing with watercolours, pastels, and charcoal

3D Art Forms

Basic modelling with clay and playdough

Music Ensembles

Rhythm patterns and simple percussion ensembles

Short Performances

Group skits and storytelling with simple costumes

Cultural Arts

Traditional crafts and music from local Kenyan culture

Collaborative Murals

Group art projects focused on community themes

Grade 3 – 4: Creative Expression

Advanced exploration and development of personal artistic style

Design Principles

Introduction to perspective and composition in visual arts

Melodic Instruments

Playing recorder, xylophone and reading basic notation

Script Writing

Character development and stage performance techniques

Cultural Dance

Cultural dance forms and basic choreography

Art Appreciation

Art critique sessions and learning about notable artists

Digital Arts

Introduction to digital drawing software and creative apps
