Admission Process:

  1. Download and complete the admission form
  2. Attach required documents (birth certificate, passport photos, previous report cards)
  3. Submit completed form to school office or via email
  4. Schedule admission interview/assessment (if required)
  5. Receive admission letter and payment instructions upon acceptance

School Uniforms

Our school uniforms promote unity, discipline, and a sense of belonging among students. All uniforms are available at the school store.

Boys Uniform

Boys' School Uniform

  • Plaid (tartan) shorts
  • White collar shirt with school badge
  • Blue V-neck sweater with school logo
  • Black leather shoes (polishable)
  • White/Blue striped socks
  • School tie (for special occasions)
  • PE Kit: Blue shorts and white t-shirt

Girls Uniform

Girls' School Uniform

  • Plaid (tartan) pinafore dress
  • White blouse with school badge
  • Blue cardigan with school logo
  • Black leather shoes (Mary Jane style)
  • White knee-high socks
  • Blue hair ribbons/bows
  • PE Kit: Blue skort and white t-shirt

Uniform Purchase: All uniforms are available at the school store located within the school premises. Opening hours: Monday-Friday 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM. Please bring your child for proper sizing.

Additional Services

We provide additional services to support our students' holistic development and convenience for parents.

Transport Services

We offer safe and reliable school transport services covering major routes in and around Kisumu. Our vehicles are well-maintained and driven by experienced, certified drivers.

  • Designated pick-up and drop-off points
  • Trained matrons on board for supervision
  • GPS-tracked vehicles for safety
  • Affordable termly transport fees
  • Flexible routes based on student needs

Meals Programme

We provide nutritious, balanced meals prepared in our modern, hygienic kitchen. Our meals programme follows guidelines from nutritionists to support children's growth and learning.

  • Breakfast and lunch provided daily
  • Special dietary needs accommodated
  • Monthly menu shared with parents
  • Emphasis on fruits and vegetables
  • Clean drinking water available throughout
