School Attire

School Uniforms

Our uniforms promote unity, discipline, and a sense of belonging. All items are available at the school store.

Boys Uniform

Boys' School Uniform

  • Plaid (tartan) shorts
  • White collar shirt with school badge
  • Blue V-neck sweater with school logo
  • Black leather shoes (polishable)
  • White/Blue striped socks
  • School tie for special occasions
  • PE Kit: Blue shorts and white t-shirt
Girls Uniform

Girls' School Uniform

  • Plaid (tartan) pinafore dress
  • White blouse with school badge
  • Blue cardigan with school logo
  • Black leather shoes (Mary Jane style)
  • White knee-high socks
  • Blue hair ribbons/bows
  • PE Kit: Blue skort and white t-shirt
Uniforms are available at the school store on premises. Opening hours: Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–4:00 PM · Sat 9:00 AM–12:00 PM. Please bring your child for accurate sizing.
Extra Care

Additional Services

We go beyond the classroom to support every learner's comfort, safety, and wellbeing.

Transport Services

Safe, reliable school transport covering major routes in and around Kisumu. Our vehicles are maintained and driven by certified, experienced drivers.

  • Designated pick-up and drop-off points
  • Trained matrons on board for supervision
  • GPS-tracked vehicles for safety
  • Affordable termly transport fees
  • Flexible routes based on student needs

Meals Programme

Nutritious, balanced meals prepared in our modern, hygienic kitchen by qualified cooks. Our menu supports children's growth, energy, and learning capacity.

  • Breakfast and lunch provided daily
  • Special dietary needs accommodated
  • Monthly menu shared with parents
  • Emphasis on fruits and vegetables
  • Clean drinking water available throughout
