Our Approach

Every Child is a Natural Scientist

At New Jets Comprehensive School, we believe curiosity is the engine of learning. Our Science & Technology programme uses the CBC framework to develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and a genuine love for discovery — from exploring nature in PP1 to simple machines in Grade 4.

Lessons are designed to be practical, collaborative, and fun. Students observe, hypothesise, experiment, and reflect — building scientific habits of mind that serve them for life.

Our Philosophy

How We Teach Science

We make science engaging, relevant, and memorable for every learner.

Hands-on Learning

Practical experiments and activities that make science come alive — students learn by doing, not just listening.

Critical Thinking

We encourage questioning, prediction, and analysis. We teach students how to think, not just what to think.

Environment Focus

Students learn to observe, care for, and protect the natural world through outdoor and nature-based activities.

Collaboration

Group projects and team-based discovery develop essential scientific communication and teamwork skills.

Ignite the Scientist Within Your Child

At New Jets Comprehensive School, every child has the opportunity to explore, experiment, and discover the wonders of the world around them.
